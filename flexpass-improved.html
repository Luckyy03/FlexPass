Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need a membership to use FlexPass?↓
No membership required at all. You pay only for the visits you make — nothing upfront, no monthly commitment. Book a slot, pay, show up.
How does payment work?↓
We support Easypaisa, JazzCash, and cash at the gym door. You never need a credit or debit card. Digital payments give you an instant QR — cash users can book and pay on arrival.
What if the gym is too crowded when I arrive?↓
Our live crowd indicator shows real-time busyness before you book. You can also get push notifications when your favourite gym quiets down — so you always know the right time to go.
Is FlexPass only for men?↓
Definitely not. We tag every partner gym with women-only hour slots. Filter your search to only show gyms offering these — so you only see spaces where you'll feel comfortable.
When does FlexPass launch?↓
We're launching first in Rawalpindi and Islamabad. Join the waitlist and you'll be the first to know — and get early access pricing before we go public.
I own a gym. How can I partner with FlexPass?↓
We'd love to work with you. FlexPass fills your empty time slots and brings new customers with zero upfront cost. Mention you're a gym owner in the waitlist form — we'll reach out first.
